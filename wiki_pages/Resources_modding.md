## Piety

In costs and effects, the game uses a standardised form of piety values to add and subtract in the form of script values. These are in the form of ``LEVEL_piety_value``, changing ``LEVEL`` to the appropriate amount of piety to add or remove:{{cite file|common\script_values\00_basic_values.txt}}

| **Level** | **Amount** |
| --- | --- |
| miniscule | 25 |
| minor | 50 |
| medium | 100 |
| major | 250 |
| massive | 500 |


Alternatively, one can use ``LEVEL_piety_gain`` or ``LEVEL_piety_loss`` to add or gain the corresponding values, respectively.
